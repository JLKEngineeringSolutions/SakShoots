About

A curious eye, a wandering mind.

I’m Josh — a Bristol-based photographer and videographer. I started in concert photography, where you learn fast how light, movement and atmosphere make a picture.

Today I shoot interiors, brands and events for venues and creative businesses across the UK. I work only with prime lenses: a deliberate limit that keeps the work clear, restrained and intentional.

Alongside commissions I keep experimenting — self-directed projects that feed back into every client shoot.
